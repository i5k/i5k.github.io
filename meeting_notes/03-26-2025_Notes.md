- **Other items from the community germane to the cc** (e.g. relevant EBP updates, new funding opportunities, meetings/conferences)
- EBP meeting updates:
  - Universal genome note system is progressing.
  - EBP life newsletter with conservation theme is released. <https://www.earthbiogenome.org/newsletter>
  - Several manuscripts released or in progress (conservation genomics, JEDI)
  - **There will be NO BG2025 meeting.** There will be a **VGP meeting at Rockefeller at the end of September**. The focus is on phase 1 VGP species, but the meeting is open to all. It will be a workshop-style meeting to solve problems.
  - VGP is creating a 'guide tree' to support vertebrate-wide genome alignment.
  - New regional node application is up on website: <https://www.earthbiogenome.org/regional-node-application>
  - Some discussion about 'associated projects' - e.g. individual labs generating 10-20 genomes that are EBP compliant. EBP is trying to formalize what these are/how they would join or submit, e.g. under a regional node.
  - Sequencing and assembly standards will be updated soon - cobionts and sex chromosomes. They are looking for new members.
  - Annotation group: working on preliminary list of metadata to provide with annotations
  - Analyses group: They're working on training activities. **The i5k training working group may want to look into what this group is doing.**
  - GoaT report - Cibele pointed out that their duplication checking tool is able to **reduce costs for projects** by detecting duplications prior to spending the money for sequencing. Will start sending updated reports to project coordinators soon.
  - Talk: Genomics of Brazilian Biodiversity (GBB). Including environmental DNA. Public-private governance
